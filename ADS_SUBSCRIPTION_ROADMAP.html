💡 Strategy — One Plan, Everything Included

❌ Selling modes separately

  • Users confused — "which plan do I need?"
  • More screens = more drop-off
  • Lower revenue per user
  • Support nightmare — "why can't I use business mode?"

✅ One Pro plan (bundles everything)

  • Simple decision — free or pro
  • Both modes + fonts + colors + no ads in one buy
  • Higher perceived value
  • Faster checkout = better conversion
Recommendation One Pro subscription unlocks Business mode, Presentation mode, all fonts, all colors, custom logo, and removes ads — all in a single purchase. Users don't need to think. The only question they ask is "is Pro worth it?" and the answer is obvious.
